### 2. Fixes (both in `orchestrator_harness/provider.py`)

**Fix 1 — default-safe bypass** (`build_argv`, was line 518): now always emits `--permission-mode`; `permission_mode = spec.permission_mode or "bypassPermissions"`. Explicit caller value still wins. Mirrors Codex's unconditional bypass, per the documented product decision.

**Fix 2 — detect permission denial** (`parse_transcript_line`, was line 538):
- `system/subtype=permission_denied` → emits `ProviderEvent("FAILED", ..., detail=<message>)` (never COMPLETED).
- Final `result` line with a **non-empty `permission_denials` list** → classified **FAILED** even when `is_error:false`/`subtype:success`; detail becomes `permission_denied Write` (tool names joined).

**Design choice (documented):** used `FAILED`, not a new `BLOCKED` kind, to stay inside the closed `PROVIDER_TERMINAL_OUTCOMES` vocabulary `{COMPLETED, FAILED, CANCELLED}` and the existing `terminal_outcome` mapping (`lane_controller.py:3143` treats anything else as `CONTROLLER_FAILED`). The result-line `permission_denials` check is the *critical* stateless signal: it guarantees the **last** terminal event is FAILED (a mid-stream FAILED alone would be overwritten by the later success result line); the mid-stream `permission_denied` event covers truncated transcripts and gives in-stream observability. No mutable state → no cross-lane race on the shared singleton adapter.

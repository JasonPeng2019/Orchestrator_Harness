# Two-hour MVP acceptance policy

Repair-gate only a reproduced defect that breaks the single supported Standard
enhanced path, its focused all-off preservation path, a credible ordinary
compatibility/recovery action those paths use, or a critical invariant:
privacy/credentials, exact task/plan/base/recipient identity, verified trust and
freshness, no fabricated success, no duplicate external mutation, durable
outcome integrity, or exact-owned cleanup.

Record every other confirmed edge, unsupported input, exhaustive mode/strategy
gap, cosmetic diagnostic, theoretical timing window, or deferred administrative
feature in `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`. It does not cause a correction or re-review.

One ideal focused test, the directly affected preservation test, and focused
critical-invariant checks are sufficient per change. A fresh reviewer returns
`REVISE` only with reproduction evidence tied to a gate above; otherwise it
returns `SHIP` and may list documented-only findings. Missing service, binding,
or credential readiness is an open dependency, never a pass.

# BEHAVIOR-01 - Continued preparation stays bounded and compatible

## Dictated outcome and source

The accepted search and trust pipeline remains usable across restart, route
correction, APC handoff, and older durable state without renewing an objective's
time or weakening exact-plan precedence. This remaining continuity outcome is
dictated by Feature Sections 6.4, 7-9, and 15 and binding Implementation Sections
2 and 12. Accepted retrieval algorithms and adapters are preserved, not specified
for reimplementation here.

## Actors, triggers, and preconditions

ROOT prepares or resumes one exact objective with task, repository baseline,
current-plan state, route/admission evidence, resolved feature and network
configuration, and trusted remaining time when known. The accepted stores and
template shortlist are optional inputs. Older supported product databases may
contain STEP-01 through STEP-03 state.

## Behavioral flow and decision rules

1. Load mandatory task and current-plan state by exact identity. An accepted
   same-objective plan wins over optional template selection. A candidate or
   reviewed same-objective plan continues its existing review unless ROOT
   explicitly replans; it is not another template/APC decision. A nonempty
   stale, unreadable, wrong-objective, or wrong-route reference is an explicit
   inconsistency.
2. Continue the same logical decision and captured configuration after a process
   restart or retry. Account for time and calls already spent. A late Level 0
   verdict abandons the topology-routed packet permanently and permits at most
   one bounded ordinary reprepare or an explicit no-memory continuation.
3. Before each remaining optional stage, including APC and final freshness,
   check its complete maximum wall allowance plus a positive execution reserve
   against current trusted remaining time. Queue, launch, model, correction,
   retrieval, exact read, scoring, collection, validation, and cancellation time
   belong to their enclosing stages. Unknown time permits only the configured
   cheap pass, not Deeper or light adaptation.
4. Keep the accepted Standard, Problem-focused, Deeper, store-fairness,
   kind-capacity, eligibility, and deterministic selection semantics. A stage
   timeout or optional outage returns completed valid results or honest no-memory
   status; it cannot enter late results after finalization.
5. Read accepted older durable records through a supported compatibility path
   without discarding decisions, reviewed experience, procedure state, or
   operations. Changed mandatory identity or policy requires explicit recovery or
   a new decision, not a retry disguised as one.

## State, data, and observable effects

The continuing decision retains exact task/objective/base/current-plan identity,
requested and effective fixed strategy, feature/network configuration,
route/supersession lineage, original deadline or elapsed-time source, spent cost,
and stage dispositions. A valid empty result is observable as no optional memory;
it does not erase exact state.

## Edge, failure, and recovery behavior

Optional timeout, missing store, malformed hit, and late result are isolated from
mandatory state. A repeated Level 0 or restart cannot replenish time indefinitely.
If a supported older schema cannot be read safely, the affected operation reports
the incompatibility and preserves the database; it does not silently create empty
replacement state. An accepted plan does not fail merely because its historical
source template is unavailable, unless unsafe current exposure is established.

## Constraints and preserved behavior

The already-accepted adapter, trust, freshness, privacy, and all-off behavior
remain authoritative. Restricted-local performs no Atlas task-path call. Fixed
strategies require no learner artifact. A concrete defect discovered at a
remaining consumer may be repaired without reopening unrelated accepted work.

## Acceptance scenarios

- Resume one partly spent decision: the same identity and configuration return,
  APC or freshness is admitted only if its full bound and reserve still fit, and
  no restart or correction resets the clock.
- A late Level 0 prevents dispatch of the old packet; one bounded ordinary
  reprepare or explicit no-memory continuation retains lineage and spent cost.
- An accepted same-objective plan bypasses template scoring, whereas a nonempty
  wrong-objective plan fails as inconsistent mandatory state.
- A candidate or reviewed same-objective plan resumes review without a second
  reuse decision; only an explicit ROOT replan permits new template/APC work.
- A database produced by accepted STEP-01 through STEP-03 opens with its decisions,
  reviewed evidence, and procedure lifecycle intact after the compatible change.
- All enhancements off makes no optional search, APC, context, or background
  service call and continues through the inherited harness path.

## Implementation freedom and unresolved decisions

Migration representation and budget bookkeeping mechanics remain implementation
choices. No product decision remains unresolved.

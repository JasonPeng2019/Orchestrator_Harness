exact collection, and verify it is absent; close the client and remove the local

recover the exact target from the run's known UUID inputs rather than broad

cleanup evidence honestly.

## Dependencies and integration

Runs whenever STEP-13 or STEP-14 created ownership, regardless of their success.
Consumes their exact identities. A clean environment does not turn a missing
behavior proof into a pass; it only closes live-safety obligations.

## Requirement-fit validation

Require readback for absence of the exact inner owner/lease, exact staged EverOS
root, and exact Atlas collection, plus preservation of unrelated owners. Then decide the MVP from the
three proof boundaries without allowing formatting, corrected intermediate
attempts, or documented-only edges to override current behavioral evidence.

**Time-crunch repair gate:** repair only a reproduced normal cleanup defect or
critical broad-deletion/ownership/privacy invariant. Document non-normal cleanup
diagnostics and other edges without correction or re-review. If cleanup cannot be
proven, expose the exact remaining owner and do not claim completion.

### Fast test suite

Use the product harness's exact scan/lease readback for the inner identity, a
filesystem absence check for the caller-owned EverOS root after handles close,
and an Atlas exact collection-existence read after drop. Rerun local cleanup unit tests
only if cleanup code changed. Run `git diff --check` on final product/planning
changes; no broad regression or packaging gate is required.

## Failure scope and recovery

An unresolved inner owner blocks reuse of that exact runtime; unresolved Atlas
cleanup blocks reuse of that exact database/collection name. Keep other evidence
and resources untouched, report the target, and retry only the exact cleanup when
the prerequisite becomes available.

### Fast lane for revisiting old work

Re-enter at the unresolved cleanup target, rerun its exact readback, then refresh
only `HANDOFF.md`/the final summary. Do not rerun implementation or behavioral
proof whose inputs did not change.

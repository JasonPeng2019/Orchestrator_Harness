# M05 - Adjudication, Acceptance, and Correction Routing

## Module contract and selection

| Module type | Decision | Instance IDs | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| M05 | SELECTED | MI-NORMAL-PRODUCT-VERDICT, MI-FL2-S2-PRODUCT-VERDICT, MI-FL2-S2-ASSET-VERDICT, MI-FL2-S2-ASSURANCE-VERDICT, MI-FL2-S2-LIVE-VERDICT, MI-NORMAL-ASSET-VERDICT, MI-NORMAL-STATIC-VERDICT, MI-NORMAL-LIVE-VERDICT | Normal and Series 2 result pools need separate ROOT semantic verdicts while preserving unaffected credit |

## Public interface and compatibility boundary

Consumes one complete result pool, frozen input, acceptance criteria, tolerances, routes, and dependency credit. Produces exactly one verdict or correction route. Changing gate question, acceptance owner, criterion, classification taxonomy, return target, or credit dependency invalidates the instance; decision mechanics do not.

## Rules, process, recipe actions, and allowed variations

For static verdict instances, REQ-021 and P11 are consumed requirements: a dispositioned initial audit with no follow-up yet takes EDGE-AUDIT-REPEAT for the uncredited audit, without M02 repair. Actual valid findings take the existing owner repair route, then the required follow-up. For normal and Series 2 live verdicts, apply BOUND-014: accept only the implementation/audit/Windows scope once proved and preserve separate macOS/Linux unverified claims; those runner gaps cannot hold EDGE-007.

P01 through P15 apply. M05 decisions are ROOT-only; its CHECKER member validates and deduplicates evidence mechanically, and the module performs no repair. Every finding receives one disposition. Compatible material findings batch once; strict test-only repair needs semantic-preservation proof; administrative faults cannot block independently decidable product results.

| Order | Recipe action ID | Project-specific action/process | Allowed parameterization | Decision owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | M05-A1 | Validate the required producer, campaign, assurance, or live result shape without treating shape as success | Configured verdict input | ROOT |
| 2 | M05-A2 | Pool and deduplicate all findings once while preserving source, severity, violated invariant, reproduction, and disagreements | One complete input pool per instance | ROOT |
| 3 | M05-A3 | Classify every follow-up as production/material, strict test-only, or administrative/support | No unclassified finding | ROOT |
| 4 | M05-A4 | For each test/support fault decide PRODUCT_INVALIDATING, NONBLOCKING_TEST_ERROR, or INDETERMINATE against affected claims | Dependency-based product impact | ROOT |
| 5 | M05-A5 | Decide each finding against required deployed behavior and proportionality; move gold-plating to the ledger | SRC-002 and SRC-003 criteria | ROOT |
| 6 | M05-A6 | Compute invalidated and preserved credit from declared dependencies | Check, review, source, runner, environment, and external inputs | ROOT |
| 7 | M05-A7 | Send each complete compatible material group once to the same M02 logical role under a newly authored correction card | Split only by owner, source context, or acceptance criterion | ROOT |
| 8 | M05-A8 | For proven strict test-only eligibility, send one complete correction card to the same M03 logical role and rerun exact affected IDs | No semantic or production change | ROOT |
| 9 | M05-A9 | Correct a consumed administrative artifact only when deterministic and cheaper than recording; otherwise record and advance nonconsumers | No product loop | ROOT |
| 10 | M05-A10 | Issue exactly one ACCEPTED, ACCEPT-WITHIN-TOLERANCE, CONTINUE, or INCOMPLETE verdict | CONTINUE only for failed or undecidable required product criterion | ROOT |

Before M05-A3 classifies a product repair, ROOT records in the existing verdict
the requirement clause, observation and exact failing assertion. In particular,
REQ-013 crash-safe complete old/new data and supported retry behavior do not by
themselves promise zero sharing errors to a non-retrying Windows reader. An uncertain
extra guarantee goes to a focused contract/feasibility inquiry before freezing a
mutation card. Inquiry findings remain open; a valid product guarantee is never
weakened merely to pass an oracle.

M05-A7 preserves the verdict's mechanism groups at dispatch. Controller/retirement
cleanup and atomic-reader feasibility are separate contexts unless ROOT identifies
one shared invariant and proof that justify combining them. Shared workflow role
is insufficient. Keep compatible findings batched, serialize overlapping source,
and carry answered questions, retained experiments and the first unresolved
executable action into each card. CHECKER's mechanical pool validation ends with
publication; ROOT alone supplies semantic classification. Apply P02/P09 to report
faults rather than reopening accepted analysis or substantive repair.

#### Outputs and results

Exactly one RESULT-PRODUCT-ACCEPTANCE: ACCEPTED, ACCEPT-WITHIN-TOLERANCE, CONTINUE, or INCOMPLETE, with all finding dispositions and credit effects.

#### Concurrency and isolation

Runs after its complete producer/campaign/assurance pool joins. It mutates no source and shares no result writer.

#### Resources and side effects

ROOT reads frozen inputs and writes one consumed verdict. No process, provider, credential, worktree, or external resource is allocated.

#### Checks and acceptance

Reconcile every selected review/check path with the frozen tip and acceptance criteria; confirm every finding has one disposition and every preserved/invalidated result has a dependency basis

#### Failure and exception routes

Material to MI-NORMAL-PRODUCT and affected product campaign; strict test-only to M03 eligibility route; administrative support stays isolated; missing decisive product fact yields INCOMPLETE

#### Prior results and change effects

Credit is invalidated only by its declared dependencies. Unrelated PASS and accepted work remain available to every nonconsumer.

#### Repeat, join, and terminal behavior

On acceptance activate EDGE-003. On material continuation author one complete correction card to MI-NORMAL-PRODUCT; strict test-only findings go only to their asset owner. CONTINUE is legal only for a failed or undecidable required product criterion and returns through a separately authored card.

#### Cost and critical-path effect

One lightweight CHECKER evidence launch followed by one ROOT decision prevents fragmented repair cycles.

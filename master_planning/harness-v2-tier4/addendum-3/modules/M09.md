## Public interface and compatibility boundary

Consumes the accepted STEP-006 test-delta coordinate and focused deterministic
verdict, claim-by-claim retained native evidence, exact provider/configuration
identity, current authority, accepted public CLI entrypoints, workspace-confined
disposable roots, and product-owned records. Produces one native-claim map whose
entries are `PROVED`, `FAILED`, or `INCOMPLETE`, plus exact cleanup state. A changed
candidate, provider adapter, profile path, hook contract, record schema, public
command, authorization, or consumed external state invalidates only claims that
consume that input.

## Rules, process, recipe actions, and allowed variations

P01 through P15 apply. ROOT owns claim selection, launch-time admission, finite
observation bounds, post-terminal record correlation, classification, and the
single join. PRACTICAL_EXECUTOR performs one predeclared native claim bundle at a
time. No background observer, custom runner, scheduler, scenario-timeout service,
prompt-generated failure fixture, or feature-by-provider matrix is authorized.

### Claim-based native closure (version 3.7)

The former 96-coordinate matrix and decision820 control apparatus are historical
only. M09 does not consume, review, integrate, rehearse, repair, or run that driver,
scheduler, observer, prompt fixture, or custom timeout logic.

The mandatory native inventory is:

1. `NATIVE-MANAGED-CODEX`, `NATIVE-MANAGED-CLAUDE`, and
   `NATIVE-MANAGED-QWEN`. For each actual provider, compatible retained PASS or one
   focused managed qualification must prove the Part XIX.1 bundle: PostToolUse;
   Stop rejects unresolved ROOT/worker state; Stop rejects a missing or invalid
   result; Stop permits a valid `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `BLOCKED` terminal result;
   managed queue-role isolation; dead/hung monitor recovery and deliberate-stop
   refusal; and serial exclusive-lease release/reuse. ROOT may share one disposable
   setup across these observations for that provider, but each assertion is recorded
   separately and no unrelated product feature is repeated.
2. `NATIVE-REAL-CORRECTION`. Compatible retained PASS or exactly one supported-CLI
   integration in a disposable Git worktree must prove invalid-result detection,
   saved-session native resume, corrected result, stable provider/session and
   lane/run identity, lease retention, prior-process cleanup, and ordinary review
   entry. The integration uses the accepted deterministic result-boundary injection
   seam to supply the fixed invalid first artifact after a real provider session is
   established; it never asks a model prompt to manufacture failure. It may be
   folded into one managed provider bundle when every assertion remains separately
   observable.
3. `NATIVE-PLAIN`. Plain mode is a grouped profile claim, not an automatic
   three-provider multiplier. Reuse compatible retained plain PASS first. Otherwise
   run exactly one authorized supported-CLI plain traversal when deterministic
   adapter evidence shows identical plain bootstrap/result/review/retire semantics.
   Add a provider-specific plain traversal only when source and accepted deterministic
   evidence identify a materially different provider-specific plain adapter or
   lifecycle seam; record that seam before launch.

Every selected bundle has one attempt. Lack of authorization, capacity,
authentication, accepted deadline basis, or required setup yields `INCOMPLETE` for
the exact native claim and no launch. A native contradiction to required product
behavior yields `FAILED` and reaches M05. The final product verdict remains separate
from the mandatory-native qualification map; EDGE-007 may deliver an honest
`INCOMPLETE` native qualification but may not label an unobserved mandatory claim
accepted.

#### Outputs and results

One RESULT-LIVE-POOL containing claim-specific retained and fresh results, plain
selection rationale, correction-integration evidence, support/native gaps, and exact
cleanup state.

#### Concurrency and isolation

Bundles run serially because they consume provider/configuration and disposable
runtime state. ROOT performs post-terminal readback directly; no observer lane or
live scheduler exists.

#### Resources and side effects

Only current user-authorized provider configurations and workspace-confined
disposable lanes/worktrees are used. Public force-stop targets only the exact owned
lane after an observation-window expiry or P15 containment trigger.

#### Checks and acceptance

CHECK-PROVIDER-CANARY is the claim map defined above. M05 and ROOT decide GATE-LIVE;
support gaps cannot become PASS and do not fabricate product failure.

#### Failure and exception routes

Product, support, evidence, timeout, and cleanup outcomes remain separate. No
automatic replay or custom-control repair follows.

#### Prior results and change effects

Credit is reused only for the exact unchanged claim inputs. A changed provider-
specific seam invalidates only its consuming claim.

#### Repeat, join, and terminal behavior

One attempt per selected bundle is joined once. A further attempt needs a new ROOT
decision after M05 classification.

#### Cost and critical-path effect

Fresh work is capped by the claim inventory: at most three managed provider bundles,
one real correction integration that may share one of those bundles, and only the
plain traversal(s) selected by the explicit material-difference rule. There is no
six-boundary minimum, sixteen-feature multiplier, or 96-coordinate replay.
